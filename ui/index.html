What this does not prove

Reserves are attested, not proven.
The contract checks that the declared reserves cover the liabilities. It cannot check that the issuer actually holds them.
You have to look.
A customer who never checks can be dropped without anyone noticing. This catches it; it does not prevent it.
Your neighbour's subtotal is visible to you.
Folding the path needs the sibling totals, so a path holder learns one other balance. Nothing reaches the chain, but it is not nothing.
