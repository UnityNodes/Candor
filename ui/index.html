What this does not prove

  1. The reserves are a claim, not a proof. The contract checks that the declared reserves cover what is owed. It cannot check that the exchange actually holds them.
  2. You have to look. Someone who never checks can be dropped and never find out. This catches it; it does not prevent it.
  3. You learn one neighbour's balance. Folding your path needs the totals beside it, so you see one other number. It never reaches the chain, but it is not nothing.
