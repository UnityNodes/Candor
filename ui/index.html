What this certificate does not certify

  1. Reserves are attested, not proven. The contract checks that the declared reserves cover the liabilities. It cannot check that the issuer holds them.
  2. The bearer has to look. A customer who never checks can be struck out without anyone noticing. This catches it; it does not prevent it.
  3. A neighbour's subtotal is legible to the bearer. Folding the path needs the sibling totals, so a path holder learns one other balance. Nothing reaches the chain, but it is not nothing.
