The exchange published

Is your money on their books?

An exchange claims it can cover everything it owes. This checks whether one person's balance is actually inside that claim.

The book the exchange published

Your balance is one leaf among 256 places. Folding it eight times has to land on exactly the root the exchange put on chain.

Public on chain, anyone can read it

Private never leaves this browser

No request goes out, not even to run the check. There is nothing to intercept and no log that records that you looked.

What this does not prove

Reserves are a claim. The contract checks the declared reserves cover what is owed. It cannot check the exchange holds them.

You have to look. Someone who never checks can be dropped and never find out. This catches it; it does not prevent it.

You see one neighbour. Folding your path needs the subtotals beside it, so you learn one other balance.
