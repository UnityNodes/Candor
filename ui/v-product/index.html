Exchange published

Pick a customer to check

The exchange claims it can cover everything it owes. This checks whether one person's balance is actually inside that claim.

The proof

Your balance is one leaf in a tree of 256 places. Folding it eight times has to land on the root the exchange published.

On this device private

None of this is transmitted, not even to run the check. There is no request to watch and no log to read.

What this does not prove

  • Reserves are a claim. The contract cannot check the exchange holds them.
  • You have to look. Someone who never checks can be dropped silently.
  • Folding your path reveals one neighbour's subtotal to you.
